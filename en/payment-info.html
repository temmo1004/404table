Payment Information

Last updated: 2026-05-19

This page describes the payment methods, course pricing, and receipt / invoice rules for 404 TABLE.

1. Payment Methods

The Service collects payment through PAYUNi Unified Payment Gateway (operated by Empower Technology Co., Ltd. — 恩沛科技股份有限公司) with Recur handling order management. Supported methods:

  • Credit cards: VISA / MasterCard / JCB (domestic and selected foreign cards)
  • Debit cards (supported by select issuers)

All credit card data is processed by PAYUNi in compliance with PCI-DSS standards. The Service does not store, transmit, or log your card number, expiration date, or CVV.

2. Course Pricing

All prices are in New Taiwan Dollars (TWD / NT$).

Single Course

NT$500 per class, one-time payment.

Includes one hour of in-person or live-online instruction, hands-on guidance from the instructor, and class templates / materials.

3. Auto-Renewal

This Service is a one-time purchase. There is no subscription and no auto-renewal. Registering for one class charges you once; future registrations require a new form submission and payment.

4. Receipts and Invoices

This Service is operated by a sole proprietor and does not issue Taiwan unified invoices (統一發票).

After each successful payment, PAYUNi will automatically email an electronic receipt (containing order ID, amount, transaction time, and the last four digits of the payment method) to the email address provided at registration. The receipt serves as a transaction and reimbursement record.

To request a replacement receipt or correct your registration email, please contact support@404table.com.

5. Cancellation and Refund

For cancellation and refund rules, see Refund Policy. Refunds may be requested per the timeline specified in that policy.

6. Payment Failures

  • If the card authorization fails, the reason will be displayed immediately. You may retry with a different card.
  • If the payment process is interrupted (browser closed, network lost), unfinished orders will not be charged. If you were charged but did not reach the payment-complete page, email us with the payment time and last four digits of the card, and customer support will investigate.

7. Customer Support

For payment-related questions, see Customer Support.
